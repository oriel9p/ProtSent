# ProteinGym: MaxSim vs pooled cosine

**Result.** On substitutions, residue-level MaxSim beats pooled cosine on *identical weights*:
+0.093 Spearman on vanilla ESM-2 35M, +0.034 on ProtSent-V2. The gain is the scoring geometry,
not our training — untrained MaxSim over native residue embeddings captures nearly all of it, and
late-interaction training adds +0.014 on ProtSent-V2 and −0.015 on ESM-2.

**Indel results are withdrawn.** A length division was applied to both scorers. MaxSim sums over
query residues so it must be divided; cosine is normalised and must not be. Constant within a
substitution group (harmless), varying within an indel group (destroys the baseline). Fixed in
`late_interaction_eval.variant_scores`; indel rows need rescoring.

Tables: `PROTEINGYM_TABLES.md`, generated by `report_proteingym.py` from the per-assay `.npz`
files. Never hand-edited.

## Protocol

`score(v) = sim(v, WT)` — mean-MaxSim over residues, or cosine over mean-pooled embeddings.
DMS: mean per-assay Spearman. Clinical: mean per-group AUC, score negated (more WT-like ⇒ less
pathogenic). Data `OATML-Markslab/ProteinGym_v1`, ≤500 variants/group, 512-residue truncation.

## Not leaderboard-comparable

Metric and aggregation now match ProteinGym's code (`performance_DMS_benchmarks.py` averages
assay → UniProt → function category; `performance_clinical_benchmarks.py` pools indels into one
AUC). Three things still differ:

- **Score.** Theirs is an LM log-likelihood ratio; ours is embedding similarity to wild type.
- **Coverage.** We drop 7 of 217 DMS assays — all long proteins (770–3033 aa) whose mutations
  fall past our truncation. Not random, and it biases us **upward**.
- **Truncation loss** by variant set: clinical_indels 15.8% · clinical_substitutions 31.8% · dms_indels 0.2% · dms_substitutions 4.5%.

For scale, leaderboard zero-shot averages ~0.40–0.52 Spearman; our best is 0.33 (0.32 corrected)
from a 35M encoder. Use these tables to rank our own arms, nothing else.

`clinical_indels` is 53 of 1,555 groups / 251 variants / median 3 per group — a per-group AUC
there can only be 0, 0.5 or 1. Smoke test, not a result.

## Findings

1. **MaxSim > cosine on identical weights.** ESM-2 35M 0.248 → 0.341 (+0.093 [+0.083, +0.104]);
   ProtSent-V2 0.282 → 0.316; late-31k 0.300 → 0.325. Only the scoring function differs.
2. **Most of it needs no training.** Trained head adds +0.014 over untrained MaxSim on
   ProtSent-V2, and −0.015 on vanilla ESM-2. Its value is compression (3.75× smaller index).
3. **Contrastive training helps cosine, hurts MaxSim.** ProtSent-V2 beats ESM-2 under cosine
   (0.282 vs 0.248) and loses under MaxSim (0.316 vs 0.341, −0.025).
4. **More pretraining splits the views.** The 31k continuation improved pooled (+0.018) and
   degraded MaxSim (−0.005; clinical −0.013), matching SCOPe (−0.015 superfamily).
5. **Size does not substitute.** ESM-2 150M cosine 0.244 ≈ 35M's 0.248; 35M MaxSim is 0.341.

Runs were unpinned (`--dataset_revision` now exists); per-arm CIs use 1,000 resamples, paired
deltas 2,000.
